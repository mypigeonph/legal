# PRIVACY POLICY FOR MY PIGEON PH

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026

**My Pigeon PH** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and National Privacy Commission (NPC) issuances.

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND PROCESS
We handle two distinct categories of data: **Cloud Account Details** and **Local Loft Records**.

### A. Cloud Account Details (Stored Online)
When you create an account, log in, or accept our legal terms, we collect and store the following basic account details in our secure cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore):
* **Display Name**
* **Email Address**
* **User ID (UID)**
* **Device ID** (used to verify your device license)
* **Legal Consent Logs** (timestamps verifying your acceptance of our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and RA 10173 notice)

### B. Local Loft Records (Stored Only on Your Phone)
**My Pigeon PH** is designed as an **offline-first application**. All your day-to-day loft features and data are processed and stored exclusively on your device's internal database (Room/SQLite):
* **Pigeon Registrations & Pedigrees:** Ring numbers, strain, color, gender, ancestral lineages (up to 6-generation PDF pedigrees), and pigeon photos.
* **Breeding & Foster Logs:** Pairings, nest box assignments, egg laying dates, 18-day hatching milestone trackers, and foster parent transfers.
* **Race & Activity Logs:** Local club race tracking, One Loft Race (OLR) records, and activity histories.
* **Health & Medication Records:** Treatments, vaccination schedules, and recovery notes.
* **Loft Financial Records:** Income, entry fees, upkeep expenses, and monthly financial reports.
* **Custom Alarms & Settings:** Daily feeding reminders and alarm settings (processed locally via Android `AlarmManager`).

> **IMPORTANT:** Your pigeon list, pedigrees, race logs, photos, and loft financial reports are **100% private to you**. They are **never** uploaded, synced, or backed up to our cloud servers.

## 2. HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED AND ENCRYPTED
* **On-Device Storage:** Your loft information is saved inside an isolated, secure database sandbox on your phone.
* **Encrypted Backups:** When you export a `.mypigeon` backup file to your phone's Downloads folder, the file is fully encrypted using password-protected **AES-256 GCM encryption**. Without your chosen password, no unauthorized person or app can read your backup file.
* **Cloud Security:** Your basic account details (Email, Display Name, UID, Device ID, and consent timestamps) are safely stored in Google Firestore with strict database security rules.

## 3. DATA SHARING
We **do not sell, rent, trade, or share** your account details, device information, or loft records with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Cloud infrastructure services (Google Firebase) are used strictly for user authentication, license validation, and legal recordkeeping.

## 4. DATA RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHT TO ERASURE
Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you retain full control over your data:
* **In-App Local Data Wipe:** Logging out, deleting your account, or switching users automatically triggers an instant, complete local database wipe. This purges all pigeon records, photos, and local settings from your device.
* **System Clearing:** Clearing app data or uninstalling the app from your Android system settings permanently removes all local databases and resets local backup states.
* **Cloud Account Erasure:** You may request the complete removal of your cloud account details (Email, Display Name, UID, Device ID, consent logs) by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

## 5. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT
Under RA 10173, you have the right to:
1. **Be Informed:** Understand how your data is collected and processed.
2. **Access & Data Portability:** Export your pigeon cards, PDF pedigrees, monthly loft financial reports, CSV imports, and encrypted `.mypigeon` backups at any time.
3. **Rectify Inaccuracies:** Update or edit your account info, bird records, and loft details directly in the app.
4. **Request Erasure:** Wipe your local device records or request cloud profile deletion.
5. **Lodge a Complaint:** Raise questions or file a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

## 6. APP PERMISSIONS REQUESTED
The app requests minimal Android system permissions:
* **INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required strictly to authenticate your account login, verify your device license, and save legal consent logs to Firestore.
* **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM:** Required to sound exact daily feeding alarms and egg hatching reminders on your device.
